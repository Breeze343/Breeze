What We Do

Our Core Services

We handle all aspects of domestic and commercial property work with expertise and diligence.

Property Maintenance

Scheduled and reactive maintenance for homes, landlords, and commercial premises. Plumbing, joinery, brickwork repairs, and general fixes.

Extensions & Alterations

Expand your living space with expertly managed single/double story house extensions, garage conversions, and internal wall removals.

Roofing & Guttering

Roof repairs, slate & tile replacement, flat roof installations (EPDM/Felt), fascia boards, and complete gutter clearing and repair.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Full refurbishment from design to installation. Tiling, plumbing, electrical installation, cabinetry, and bespoke finishes.

Painting & Decorating

Interior and exterior painting services using high grade, durable weather-resistant paints with immaculate surface preparation.

Patios & Exterior Work

Paving, stone patio installation, garden walling, fencing, pressure washing, and structural landscaping.
